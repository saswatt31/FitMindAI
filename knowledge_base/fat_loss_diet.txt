FAT LOSS DIET GUIDE

CALORIE DEFICIT FUNDAMENTALS
To lose fat, you must consume fewer calories than you burn. A deficit of 300-500 calories per day leads to approximately 0.3-0.5 kg of fat loss per week. Avoid deficits greater than 700 calories as this causes muscle loss and metabolic slowdown.

MACRONUTRIENT TARGETS FOR FAT LOSS
- Protein: 1.8-2.5g per kg of bodyweight. High protein preserves muscle mass during a deficit and keeps you satiated.
- Carbohydrates: 2-3g per kg of bodyweight. Prioritize complex carbs like oats, brown rice, sweet potato, and quinoa.
- Fats: 0.6-1g per kg of bodyweight. Include healthy fats from olive oil, avocado, nuts, and fatty fish.

MEAL TIMING FOR FAT LOSS
- Eat 3-5 meals spread throughout the day to manage hunger.
- Place your largest carbohydrate meal around your workout (pre or post).
- Consume a protein-rich meal before bed to reduce overnight muscle breakdown.
- Avoid eating large meals within 2 hours of sleep.

BEST FOODS FOR FAT LOSS
High Volume, Low Calorie: leafy greens, cucumber, zucchini, broccoli, cauliflower, bell peppers, mushrooms.
Lean Proteins: chicken breast, turkey, white fish (tilapia, cod), egg whites, low-fat Greek yogurt, cottage cheese.
Smart Carbs: oats, brown rice, lentils, chickpeas, sweet potato, whole wheat bread.
Healthy Fats: avocado (half per day), almonds, walnuts, olive oil (1-2 tbsp), salmon.

FOODS TO LIMIT DURING FAT LOSS
- Refined sugars: sodas, candy, pastries, white bread.
- Fried foods and fast food.
- Alcohol (7 calories per gram, lowers fat oxidation).
- High-calorie sauces: mayonnaise, ranch, BBQ sauce.

SAMPLE FAT LOSS DAY PLAN (1800 kcal)
Breakfast (400 kcal): 4 egg whites + 1 whole egg scrambled, 1 cup oats with berries, black coffee.
Snack (150 kcal): 1 cup Greek yogurt (0% fat) with cinnamon.
Lunch (450 kcal): 150g grilled chicken breast, 1 cup brown rice, large salad with balsamic dressing.
Pre-workout (100 kcal): 1 banana.
Post-workout (300 kcal): Protein shake with milk and 1 tbsp peanut butter.
Dinner (400 kcal): 150g white fish, 2 cups roasted vegetables, 1/2 cup quinoa.

HYDRATION FOR FAT LOSS
- Drink 3-4 liters of water per day.
- Drink 500ml of water before each meal to reduce appetite.
- Green tea (2-3 cups daily) can mildly boost metabolism.

COMMON FAT LOSS MISTAKES
1. Not tracking calories - most people underestimate intake by 30-50%.
2. Cutting carbs too aggressively - leads to low energy and poor workouts.
3. Skipping protein - causes muscle loss and increased hunger.
4. Doing only cardio - resistance training is essential for maintaining muscle.
5. Weekend overeating - undoes the weekly deficit achieved on weekdays.
