VEGETARIAN AND VEGAN PROTEIN SOURCES GUIDE

COMPLETE VS INCOMPLETE PROTEINS
Complete proteins contain all 9 essential amino acids. Most plant proteins are incomplete, but combining different plant sources throughout the day ensures you get all amino acids.
Complete plant proteins: quinoa, soy (tofu, tempeh, edamame), buckwheat, hemp seeds, chia seeds.
Complementary pairs: rice + lentils, bread + peanut butter, hummus + whole wheat pita.

TOP VEGETARIAN PROTEIN SOURCES

LEGUMES AND PULSES
- Lentils (red, green, black): 18g protein per cooked cup, 230 kcal. Also rich in iron and fiber.
- Chickpeas: 15g protein per cooked cup, 270 kcal. Versatile in curries, salads, or roasted.
- Black beans: 15g protein per cooked cup. High in fiber and antioxidants.
- Kidney beans: 13g protein per cooked cup. Great in stews and chili.
- Edamame: 17g protein per cup, 180 kcal. Eat as a snack or add to stir-fries.

SOY PRODUCTS
- Tempeh: 31g protein per 100g, 195 kcal. Fermented, great gut health benefits. Best animal protein substitute.
- Firm Tofu: 17g protein per 100g, 144 kcal. Absorbs flavors well, great grilled or scrambled.
- Silken Tofu: 6g protein per 100g. Blend into smoothies or use in desserts.
- Soy milk: 7g protein per cup, 80 kcal. Good for post-workout shakes.

DAIRY AND EGGS (Lacto-Ovo Vegetarian)
- Greek Yogurt (0% fat): 17g protein per 170g serving, 100 kcal. Excellent breakfast or snack.
- Cottage Cheese: 25g protein per cup, 200 kcal. Great before bed (slow-digesting casein).
- Eggs: 6g protein per large egg, 70 kcal. Highly bioavailable. Eat whole eggs for best nutrition.
- Paneer: 18g protein per 100g. Popular in Indian cooking, great for high-protein curries.
- Whey Protein Powder: 24-27g protein per scoop. Fast-digesting, ideal post-workout.

GRAINS AND SEEDS
- Quinoa: 8g protein per cooked cup, 222 kcal. Complete protein, gluten-free.
- Hemp Seeds: 10g protein per 3 tbsp, 166 kcal. Rich in omega-3 fatty acids.
- Chia Seeds: 5g protein per 2 tbsp. Also provides omega-3s and calcium.
- Pumpkin Seeds: 9g protein per 28g serving. Rich in zinc and magnesium.
- Nutritional Yeast: 8g protein per 2 tbsp, 45 kcal. Cheesy flavor, rich in B12.

MEAT SUBSTITUTES
- Seitan (wheat gluten): 25g protein per 100g, 370 kcal. Excellent texture, very high protein.
- Mycoprotein (Quorn): 11g protein per 100g. Great texture, low in fat.

HIGH-PROTEIN VEGETARIAN MEAL IDEAS
Breakfast: Greek yogurt parfait with hemp seeds and berries (25g protein)
Lunch: Quinoa and black bean bowl with roasted vegetables (22g protein)
Dinner: Tempeh stir-fry with brown rice and edamame (35g protein)
Snack: Cottage cheese with almonds and fruit (28g protein)
Post-Workout: Whey protein shake with soy milk and banana (35g protein)

HITTING PROTEIN TARGETS AS A VEGETARIAN
For 75kg person targeting 150g protein daily:
- Breakfast: Greek yogurt + eggs = 35g
- Lunch: Lentil soup + quinoa = 30g
- Snack: Cottage cheese = 25g
- Post-workout: Protein shake = 27g
- Dinner: Tempeh + edamame = 33g
Total: 150g protein

IRON AND B12 FOR VEGETARIANS
- Iron: Eat iron-rich foods (lentils, spinach, tofu) with vitamin C to improve absorption.
- Vitamin B12: Only found naturally in animal products. Vegetarians should supplement or consume fortified foods.
- Zinc: Pumpkin seeds, hemp seeds, legumes, and nutritional yeast are good sources.
- Omega-3: Flaxseeds, chia seeds, hemp seeds, walnuts. Consider algae-based omega-3 supplement.
