MUSCLE GAIN DIET GUIDE

CALORIE SURPLUS FOR MUSCLE GROWTH
To build muscle, you need a calorie surplus of 200-400 calories above your TDEE. A lean bulk (small surplus) minimizes fat gain while maximizing muscle. Dirty bulking (large surplus) adds unnecessary fat and is not recommended.

MACRONUTRIENT TARGETS FOR MUSCLE GAIN
- Protein: 1.8-2.2g per kg of bodyweight. Essential for muscle protein synthesis. Spread across 4-5 meals.
- Carbohydrates: 4-6g per kg of bodyweight. Fuel for intense workouts and glycogen replenishment.
- Fats: 0.8-1.2g per kg of bodyweight. Support hormone production including testosterone.

MEAL TIMING FOR MUSCLE GAIN
- Pre-workout meal (1-2 hours before): carbs + protein. Example: rice and chicken.
- Post-workout meal (within 30-60 minutes): fast carbs + protein. Example: protein shake with banana.
- Eat every 3-4 hours to maintain positive nitrogen balance.
- Never skip breakfast - start protein synthesis early in the day.

BEST FOODS FOR MUSCLE GAIN
High-Quality Proteins: chicken breast, beef (85% lean), eggs (whole), salmon, tuna, whey protein, casein protein, Greek yogurt.
Quality Carbs: white rice (fast digesting), oats, sweet potato, pasta, whole grain bread, fruits (bananas, apples).
Healthy Fats: whole eggs, fatty fish, nuts, olive oil, avocado.
Muscle-Building Extras: creatine monohydrate (5g daily), whole milk, nut butters.

SAMPLE MUSCLE GAIN DAY PLAN (3000 kcal for 75kg person)
Breakfast (600 kcal): 4 whole eggs scrambled, 2 slices whole wheat toast, 1 cup oats with banana and honey.
Mid-Morning (400 kcal): Protein shake (40g protein) with whole milk, 1 apple.
Lunch (700 kcal): 200g chicken breast, 1.5 cups white rice, mixed vegetables with olive oil.
Pre-Workout (300 kcal): 2 slices bread with peanut butter and jam, 1 banana.
Post-Workout (500 kcal): Whey protein shake, 1 cup oats with honey.
Dinner (500 kcal): 200g salmon, 1 cup sweet potato mash, large salad.

PROGRESSIVE OVERLOAD AND NUTRITION
- Increase calories slightly every 2-3 weeks if the scale isn't moving up.
- If gaining more than 0.5kg/week, reduce calories by 100-200 to limit fat gain.
- Track your lifts - if strength isn't increasing, you're likely undereating.

SUPPLEMENTS FOR MUSCLE GAIN
1. Creatine Monohydrate: 5g daily, proven to increase strength and muscle mass.
2. Whey Protein: convenient protein source, take post-workout.
3. Caffeine: improves workout performance. 200-400mg pre-workout.
4. Vitamin D3: supports testosterone and immune function.

SLEEP AND RECOVERY
- Muscle is built during rest, not during the workout.
- Aim for 7-9 hours of sleep per night.
- A casein protein shake before bed (cottage cheese or casein powder) feeds muscles overnight.
