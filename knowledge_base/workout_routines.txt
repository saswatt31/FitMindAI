WORKOUT ROUTINES GUIDE

TRAINING SPLITS BY AVAILABLE DAYS

2 DAYS PER WEEK - FULL BODY
Best for: beginners, people with busy schedules.
Day 1 (Monday): Squat 3x8, Bench Press 3x8, Bent-over Row 3x8, Overhead Press 3x8, Plank 3x30s
Day 2 (Thursday): Deadlift 3x6, Incline Press 3x8, Pull-ups 3x8, Dumbbell Lunges 3x10, Cable Row 3x10

3 DAYS PER WEEK - PUSH/PULL/LEGS (PPL)
Best for: intermediate lifters wanting balanced development.
Push Day (Chest, Shoulders, Triceps):
- Bench Press 4x8, Overhead Press 3x8, Incline Dumbbell Press 3x10
- Lateral Raises 3x15, Tricep Pushdowns 3x12, Dips 3x10

Pull Day (Back, Biceps):
- Deadlift 4x5, Pull-ups 4x8, Barbell Row 3x8
- Cable Row 3x10, Face Pulls 3x15, Barbell Curl 3x10

Leg Day (Quads, Hamstrings, Glutes, Calves):
- Squat 4x8, Romanian Deadlift 3x10, Leg Press 3x12
- Leg Curl 3x12, Walking Lunges 3x10 each, Calf Raises 4x15

4 DAYS PER WEEK - UPPER/LOWER SPLIT
Best for: intermediate to advanced lifters.
Upper A (Monday): Bench Press 4x6, Row 4x6, Overhead Press 3x8, Pull-ups 3x8, Curl 3x10, Tricep Extension 3x10
Lower A (Tuesday): Squat 4x6, Romanian Deadlift 3x8, Leg Press 3x10, Leg Curl 3x10, Calf Raises 4x15
Upper B (Thursday): Incline Press 4x8, Cable Row 4x8, Dumbbell Shoulder Press 3x10, Lat Pulldown 3x10
Lower B (Friday): Deadlift 4x5, Front Squat 3x8, Bulgarian Split Squat 3x8, Leg Extension 3x12, Calf Raises 4x15

5 DAYS PER WEEK - PUSH/PULL/LEGS/UPPER/LOWER (PPL+)
Best for: advanced lifters who have been training 2+ years.
Monday: Push, Tuesday: Pull, Wednesday: Legs, Thursday: Upper, Friday: Lower, Weekend: Rest

6 DAYS PER WEEK - ARNOLD SPLIT
Chest+Back / Shoulders+Arms / Legs — repeated twice per week.
Only for advanced lifters with excellent recovery and nutrition.

FAT LOSS SPECIFIC CARDIO
LISS (Low Intensity Steady State):
- 30-45 minutes walking (brisk), cycling, or swimming at 60-65% max heart rate.
- Best done in the morning fasted or post-workout.
- 3-5 sessions per week for fat loss.

HIIT (High Intensity Interval Training):
- 20-25 minutes total, alternating 40 seconds hard effort + 20 seconds rest.
- Burns more calories in less time, boosts metabolism for hours.
- Limit to 2-3 sessions per week to avoid overtraining.
- Exercises: sprints, bike sprints, jump rope, burpees, mountain climbers.

PROGRESSIVE OVERLOAD PRINCIPLES
- Add 2.5kg (5 lbs) to the bar every week for compound lifts when you can complete all sets.
- If you fail to complete reps, stay at the same weight until you can.
- After 3 failed attempts at the same weight, drop by 10% and rebuild.
- Track every workout in a notebook or app.

WARM-UP PROTOCOL (Do this before every session)
1. 5 minutes light cardio (walk/jog/bike)
2. Dynamic stretching: leg swings, arm circles, hip rotations (2 minutes)
3. Activation exercises: band pull-aparts, glute bridges, bodyweight squats (2 sets each)
4. Warm-up sets: 50% of working weight x 10 reps, then 75% x 5 reps

RECOVERY AND REST
- Muscle groups need 48-72 hours of recovery between sessions.
- Sleep 7-9 hours per night — growth hormone is released during deep sleep.
- Active recovery: light walking, yoga, or swimming on rest days.
- Deload every 8-12 weeks: reduce weight by 40-50% for one week to allow full recovery.

Push (Chest/Shoulders/Triceps): Push-ups 4x15, Pike Push-ups 3x12, Diamond Push-ups 3x10, Dips on chair 3x12

Legs: Bodyweight Squats 4x20, Bulgarian Split Squats 3x12 each, Glute Bridges 4x15, Calf Raises 4x25
Core: Plank 3x45s, Bicycle Crunches 3x20, Leg Raises 3x15, Mountain Climbers 3x30s
